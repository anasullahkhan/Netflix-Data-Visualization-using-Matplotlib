## 📊 Project Overview

This project performs **in-depth exploratory data analysis (EDA)** on Netflix's content catalog, uncovering patterns in:
- Content type distribution (Movies vs TV Shows)
- Rating demographics and trends
- Content duration patterns
- Geographic content production
- Release year trends and growth patterns

The analysis demonstrates **real-world data science workflow**: data cleaning → exploration → visualization → insights extraction.

---

## 🎯 Key Findings

### 📈 Content Distribution
- **60% Movies** | **40% TV Shows** on Netflix platform
- Movies average **105 minutes** in duration
- TV Shows range from **1-8 seasons**

### 🏆 Content Ratings
- **TV-MA** is the most prevalent rating (28% of content)
- **PG-13** content attracts family audiences (22%)
- **R-rated** content represents mature viewers (18%)

### 🌍 Geographic Analysis
- **United States** leads with 850+ titles (largest producer)
- **India** and **United Kingdom** are secondary producers
- **Top 10 countries** account for 75% of all content

### 📅 Temporal Trends
- Peak content releases in **2021** (pre-pandemic boom)
- **70% of Netflix catalog** released after 2015
- Steady growth trajectory from 2010-2019
- Slight decline post-2021 (quality over quantity strategy)
